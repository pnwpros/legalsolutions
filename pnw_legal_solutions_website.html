Frequently Asked Questions

How does the sliding scale hourly rate work?

Our fees are directly tied to your annual household income. If you earn between $0 and $25,000, your rate is $100/hr. For $25,001–$50,000 it is $150/hr, for $50,001–$74,999 it is $200/hr, and for $75,000+ it is $250/hr. You select your bracket when booking your consultation.

Why is the consultation fee non-refundable?

To maintain our affordable sliding-scale rates and ensure dedicated attorney preparation, conflict checks, and document review for every scheduled appointment, consultation fees are strictly non-refundable once booked.

Can Washington State court fines and LFOs really be waived?

Yes. Under Washington law and recent court rulings (such as RCW 10.01.160), courts have the authority to remit or waive legal financial obligations, interest, and non-mandatory fees if payment imposes a manifest hardship. We prepare formal motions demonstrating indigency or financial burden.

What is required to restore firearm rights in Washington?

Restoration depends on the nature of the conviction (felony vs. certain misdemeanors/domestic violence offenses), the completion of all sentencing requirements, and statutory waiting periods (typically 3 to 5+ years crime-free in the community). We review your criminal history report to confirm eligibility.
